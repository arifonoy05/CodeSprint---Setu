# RCA-1092 — Disbursement to a closed borrower account

**Severity:** High
**Module:** Loan Disbursement
**Raised:** 2025-02-27
**Closed:** 2025-03-06

## Summary
Funds were posted to a borrower account closed six weeks earlier. The core banking system accepted
the posting into a suspense account rather than rejecting it.

## Root cause
`loan.borrower_account` is captured at application time and never revalidated. Neither `disburse()`
nor `checkEligibility()` verifies that the account is still open at the moment of disbursement, and
the ledger client treats any 2xx response as success without inspecting the posting status returned
in the body.

## Preventive action
**Open.** Account status must be revalidated at disbursement, and the ledger response body inspected.
