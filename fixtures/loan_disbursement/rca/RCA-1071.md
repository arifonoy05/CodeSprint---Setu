# RCA-1071 — Ledger posting succeeded, transaction row lost

**Severity:** Critical
**Module:** Loan Disbursement
**Raised:** 2024-09-19
**Closed:** 2024-10-01

## Summary
Money reached the borrower's account but the module had no record of the disbursement. The loan
remained in `APPROVED` status and was disbursed a second time three days later.

## What happened
The application pod was restarted by the platform team mid-request, after `postToLedger()` returned
but before `attachLedgerRef()` and `markDisbursed()` completed.

## Root cause
`disburse()` performs a sequence of writes — insert transaction, call an external system, update the
transaction, build the schedule, update loan status — with no transaction boundary and no
compensating action. The external ledger call cannot be rolled back, and nothing detects a
half-completed disbursement afterwards.

## Preventive action
**Open.** Needs an outbox or a reconciliation sweep for transactions with a null `ledger_ref`.
