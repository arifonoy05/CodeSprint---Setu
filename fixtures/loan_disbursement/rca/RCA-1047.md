# RCA-1047 — Repayment due dates falling on public holidays

**Severity:** High
**Module:** Loan Disbursement / Repayment Schedule
**Raised:** 2024-05-02
**Closed:** 2024-05-19

## Summary
Roughly 4,100 loans generated installment due dates falling on public holidays and weekends. Auto
debit failed on those dates, and the overnight batch flagged the accounts as overdue, briefly
placing performing loans in the NPA reporting bucket.

## What happened
Schedules generated in April placed due dates on Eid holidays. Collections could not be attempted
until the following working day, but the delinquency batch had already run.

## Root cause
`buildSchedule()` computes each due date with `addMonths()` and writes it directly. The
`holiday_calendar` table exists and is maintained by Operations, but nothing in schedule generation
reads it. There is no business-day adjustment rule of any kind.

## Resolution
Affected schedules were regenerated manually and the delinquency flags reversed.

## Preventive action
**Open.** A business-day adjustment convention needs to be agreed with the business — whether to
move forward, backward, or to the following month-end.
