# RCA-1104 — EMI rounding drift over long tenors

**Severity:** Low
**Module:** Repayment Schedule
**Raised:** 2025-04-09
**Closed:** 2025-04-30

## Summary
For 240-month loans, the sum of scheduled installments differed from principal plus interest by up
to 180 BDT. Borrowers disputed the final installment.

## Root cause
`calculateEmi()` returns an unrounded float and every schedule row stores the same value. No
residual adjustment is applied to the final installment, and `numeric(18,2)` truncation in
`loan_schedule.installment` compounds across rows.

## Preventive action
**Open.** Needs an agreed rounding convention and a residual adjustment on the last installment.
