# RCA-1052 — Reversal leaves orphan disbursement transaction

**Severity:** High
**Module:** Loan Disbursement / Finance
**Raised:** 2024-06-11
**Closed:** 2024-07-02

## Summary
When Finance reverses a disbursement, the ledger entry is reversed but the `disbursement_txn` row
remains. Reconciliation reports a permanent variance between the module and the general ledger.

## What happened
Finance reversed a disbursement made against the wrong loan account. The reversal is performed in
the core banking system by Finance, entirely outside this module. Nothing notifies the loan module.

## Root cause
There is no reversal path in the disbursement module at all. Reversal is a Finance-side operation
by design, but no callback, event or reconciliation job exists to bring the loan module back into
agreement. The loan also remains in `DISBURSED` status with a `disbursed_amount` that is no longer
true.

## Resolution
Monthly manual reconciliation spreadsheet, maintained by Finance.

## Preventive action
**Open.** Needs an agreed contract for how Finance notifies the module of a reversal.
